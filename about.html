When I started Branquinho Studio, I was about 11 years old, but it wasn’t just some silly game—I really wanted to build a business. However, back then I didn’t know how to do much, so my options were limited. Basically, from 2020 through early 2025, all I did for Branquinho Studio was draw boring comic strips and make short videos, and then show them all to my friends.

But then, in 2025, things changed. This time, I really wanted to give Branquinho Studio a chance, so I put together a development team with Marcos, who in turn brought in a guy named Jonas, and then we all started developing some games. That’s how Branquinho Studio first started to gain recognition among a few people who actually played one of the games we created!
